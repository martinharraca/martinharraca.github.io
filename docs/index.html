Martín Harracá

About

I am an economist (University of Buenos Aires), with an MA in Political and Economic Analysis (Université Paris 13) and a PhD in Business and Management from the University of Surrey. I am a researcher and lecturer at the School of Economics and Business, UNSAM (Argentina), and an associate researcher at SEDLab (ICC, CONICET–UBA) and at Surrey’s Centre for Digital Economy. In 2026/27 I am a Kistefos Fellow at the St.Gallen Collegium, within its annual programme on digital sovereignty.

My research looks at strategy, competition and governance in digital platforms, with work published in Research Policy and Business Ethics Quarterly. I also build simulation models for development planning, including the Productive Digital Transformation Simulator (ECLAC–GIZ) and the TED&POP model.

Before that I spent over fifteen years in public management and consulting: I coordinated the Directorate of Models and Projections at Argentina’s Ministry of Economy and was Head of Planning and Management Control at the General Directorate of Military Industries.
