For journalists and editors: what I can speak on, a short bio and past coverage.

Topics I can speak on

  • Platform power — how Amazon, Mercado Libre and others shape the rules for sellers and consumers
  • Tech regulation — what Latin America can learn from Europe’s DMA, and Brazil’s approach
  • Digital sovereignty — who owns the cloud and why it matters for countries
  • AI and the economy — competition, concentration and who wins
  • Evidence for policy — using simulation to test policies before applying them
  • Work and inequality — gig work, gender and the platform economy

Short bio

Martín Harracá is an economist (UBA) with a PhD from the University of Surrey. He researches digital platforms and development policy at UNSAM, Argentina, and is a 2026/27 Kistefos Fellow at the St.Gallen Collegium.
