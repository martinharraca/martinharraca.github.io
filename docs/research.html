Research

Three themes and the projects I am working on now.

§ 01

Platform competition, power, and governance

Platforms write and enforce the rules for the sellers, developers and workers who depend on them. I study how that governance works in practice: what happens among complementors when rules are applied inconsistently, and how platform power reaches beyond markets to shape the autonomy of those who use them.

Key work
  • Inconsistent platform governance and social contagion of misconduct in digital ecosystems: A complementors perspective · 2025
  • How Digital Platforms Organize Immaturity: A Sociosymbolic Framework of Platform Power · 2023
  • Essays on Power in Digital Platforms · 2023
§ 02

Digital sovereignty and development from the periphery

The platforms that organise Latin America’s digital markets are governed by a small group of firms. I study what room countries in the periphery have to engage with these firms through public policy. My fellowship project at the St.Gallen Collegium looks at Amazon and Mercado Libre in Mexico, Brazil and Argentina.

Key work
  • La economía política de las plataformas y el rol estratégico de la política pública · 2023
  • New valorization logics in the figure of the digital platform. The case of MercadoLibre · 2022
  • Economía de plataformas: un estado de situación · 2021
§ 03

Simulation for development planning

With colleagues at SEDLab I build computational models to design and test policies before they are applied: the Productive Digital Transformation Simulator (SimTDP), developed with ECLAC and GIZ, and TED&POP, a stock-flow consistent model of the development trilemma in peripheral economies.

Key work
  • Simulador de la transformación digital productiva: una herramienta para el diseño de políticas basadas en datos · 2026
  • Simulación de un trilema del desarrollo en economías periféricas con el modelo TED&POP: Telares, Dólares, Ponchos y Pesos · 2026
  • A Policy-Planning Simulation Platform for Productive Digital Transformation in Latin America and the Caribbean · 2026
  • Simulating a Development Trilemma in Peripheral Economies: Growth, Distribution, and External Constraints in a Stock-Flow Consistent Model · 2025
